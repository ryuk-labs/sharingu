# Tea Shop — the product brief

## Aim
Sell loose-leaf tea online to people who come back every month.
Measure: repeat orders a month = 40 (September 2026), from the orders table

## Goals
- tea-shop-live: The web shop takes orders [leader]
  - annual-plan: An annual plan on the pricing page [code]
  - fast-checkout: Checkout in under a minute [design]

## Start here
Tea Shop is a small web shop: Home, Pricing and Checkout, over two API doors (prices and orders).
Read the aim first, then the goals; the open questions on Decisions are what the team has not settled yet.
